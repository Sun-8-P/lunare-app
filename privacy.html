Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

Lunare ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Lunare is designed with privacy in mind. All app data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our servers.

We use the following third-party services which may collect limited data:

  • RevenueCat — manages subscription state. May collect anonymized purchase data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • PostHog — anonymized analytics to help us understand how the app is used. No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Google AdMob — serves ads in the free version. May collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All user data and app progress is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

Lunare is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
